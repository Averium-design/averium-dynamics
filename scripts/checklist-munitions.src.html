What to settle before a deployment

Five points need to be settled, whichever supplier is involved.

What to settleBasis
Operating category: dropping objects and dangerous goods are not permitted in the open categoryRegulation (EU) 2019/947
Exemption for the BOS, including third parties acting on their behalf§ 21k LuftVO, EGRED 2 (BBK, 2nd edition 2023), STS-BOS (LBA, 16 July 2025)
Coordination with crewed aviation, visibility via ADS-B or FLARMDFV and AGBF, Fachempfehlung Drohnen und UAV, August 2025
Extinguishing agent: placing on the market, market surveillance, permit, storage, transportProdSG, § 7 SprengG, 1. SprengV
Failed drop: what happens to a payload that has not gone off, on ground nobody may enterDeployment planning

Disclosure: Averium Dynamics develops firefighting drones. The five points above apply to us as they do to any other supplier.
